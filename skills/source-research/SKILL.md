# Source research

Research existing material with web search. Start with practitioner reports, professional communities, implementation case studies and direct operational experience; then locate primary research and authoritative documentation. Seek credible counterarguments and failures, not only confirmation. Search in English and in at least two relevant non-English languages (select languages relevant to the topic/audience). Prefer dated, attributable sources. Record source title, publisher, date when available, direct URL, what it supports and limitations. Never fabricate URLs, quotations or findings. Clearly separate externally verified claims from hypotheses/inferences. Flag unsupported manuscript claims and research gaps. Preserve meaningful disagreement and practitioner texture (Diversity Maintainer principle).
